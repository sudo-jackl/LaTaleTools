How sprite coordinates work

A frame is one sprite image or part of a pose. An atlas packs several frames into one PNG. This example follows the same 40 × 60 pixel crop from the atlas into its original frame.

1. Crop from the atlas

PosTopLeft: (60, 20) PosBottomRight: (100, 80) Crop size: 40 × 60 pixels

2. Place in the original frame

Offset: (20, 30) CenterOffset: (−30, −20) Pivot within crop: (30, 20)
Frame origin → crop Frame center → crop X → right; Y ↓ down

Offset places the crop relative to the original frame's top-left. CenterOffset places it relative to that frame's center; negate it to find the rotation pivot within the crop. Negative offsets are valid. These example values illustrate the fields; they are not taken from the currently opened TBL.
